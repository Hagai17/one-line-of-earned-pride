A tiny Python program that writes one line of earned pride to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 240 characters holding a quiet, earned moment of pride — a man eleven days sober who holds his brother's champagne through the wedding toast, raises it with everyone else, and hands it back full. The line was picked by drafting fourteen candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — genuine feeling first, then a concrete scene with a real person in it, pride earned inside the line by effort the reader can feel, a landing in one read, and freshness over the stock trophy, podium or graduation. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
